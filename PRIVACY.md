# Privacy

ExtBridge does not collect, store or send any personal data.

* The app and its file system module run entirely on your Mac. They do not contact any server.
* Settings and the status of your disks are kept in the app's own container on your Mac.
* Diagnostic messages go to the macOS system log on your Mac only.
* The "buy me a coffee" button opens PayPal or Stripe in your browser (download version) or uses
  Apple's in-app purchase (App Store version). ExtBridge does not see your payment details; the
  payment provider's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Questions: open an issue on the project page.
